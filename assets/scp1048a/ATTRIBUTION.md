Processing (2026-07-24, for the SCP-1048-A scatter template): uniform-scaled to a 0.065 m ear
length, solidified 3 mm (the source is an open scan sheet), voxel-remeshed at 2.2 mm into a
single closed shell, decimated 8064 → 120 tris, re-oriented with the relief facing **+Z** and
the flat solidified back on the **z = 0** plane, silhouette centred on X/Y. No source textures
retained.
