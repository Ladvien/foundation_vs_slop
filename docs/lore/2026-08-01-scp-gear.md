# Foundation vs. Slop — Equipment List (30) with Image Prompts

Thirty pieces of field equipment, built to plug into the game's systems: each is tagged with the **party role** it serves and, where relevant, the **containment archetype** or **loop hook** it maps to (single-target capture / area-denial quarantine / source-elimination; research economy; Thaumiel unlock). Descriptions are game-facing; image prompts are asset-generation-ready.

**Licensing tags** — because CC BY-SA 3.0 share-alike is viral for canon content (not legal advice):
- **(canon)** — established SCP-wiki equipment; using it as-is carries attribution + share-alike obligations.
- **(canon-inspired)** — based on a canon concept but renamed/adapted for the game; lower exposure, still courteous to credit the idea.
- **(original)** — invented here for the game; yours free and clear.

**House style (append to any single prompt for consistency):**
> *SCP Foundation field equipment, near-future clinical-industrial design; worn matte olive-drab, gunmetal, off-white polymer and brushed steel; stenciled black serial numbers and hazard labels; black rubber grips; studio concept-sheet product shot on a seamless neutral-grey background, three-quarter orthographic view, soft key light with subtle rim light, physically-based game-asset render; muted desaturated palette so any anomalous glow reads as the only saturated color in frame.*

Accent-glow convention used below: **reality/Hume tech → cold cyan-white · psionic → magenta · thaumaturgic/divine → amber-gold runes · bio/xeno → pale green · mundane/kinetic → no glow.**

## Researcher — detection & measurement

**1. Kant Counter** *(canon · reality detection)*
Handheld meter that reads the local **Hume** level — how much "reality" is present in an area. It spikes near reality-benders and craters in dead zones; in-game it's the researcher's core read on the stability meter and tells you whether a containment basin is holding.
*Image prompt:* A rugged handheld reality meter shaped like a boxy analog Geiger counter, brushed-steel casing with an amber segmented Nixie-style readout and a coiled probe wand, a small cold-cyan calibration aperture glowing on one side.

**2. Cayce Counter** *(canon · psionic detection)*
Detects the presence, direction, and strength of **psionic fields**. It points at your own psionic operative as readily as at a threat — a quiet reminder of who, on the team, is also a liability.
*Image prompt:* A slim directional wand scanner with a fan of thin antenna prongs and a circular magenta phosphor dial, matte off-white polymer body, single magenta indicator LED, wrist strap; magenta accent glow.

**3. Kardec Spectral Analyzer** *(canon · ectoplasm detection)*
Reads ectoplasmic and spectral residue, flagging incorporeal anomalies before they fully manifest. Pairs with the nPDN (#11) to force ghosts solid.
*Image prompt:* A boxy field spectrometer with a swiveling parabolic sensor dish and a green oscilloscope screen showing a jittering waveform, worn gunmetal housing with rubber corner bumpers; faint pale-green accent glow.

**4. Anomaly Field Slate** *(original · field visualization · GAME CORE)*
A ruggedized tablet that renders the invisible **stigmergic fields** (attention, threat, scent, alarm) as a live color overlay on the environment — the operator's only window into the substrate that drives every creature and every capture.
*Image prompt:* A thick armored field tablet in an olive-drab rubber case with reinforced corners and a hand-strap, its screen showing a topographic heat-map overlay in cyan-to-amber gradients, chunky physical buttons along the bezel; screen-glow accent.

**5. Antimemetic Ocular Rig** *(canon-inspired · antimeme perception)*
Mnestic-dosing goggles that let the wearer see and *remember* antimemetic anomalies — the things an unaugmented mind erases the instant it looks away. Runs on a timed mnestic cartridge.
*Image prompt:* Heavy sealed goggles with twin thick lenses and a side-mounted drug-cartridge port holding a half-full amber vial, matte-black rubber face seal and adjustable straps, a faint reticle etched into the glass; subtle flickering violet lens reflection.

**6. Provenance Scanner** *(original · research economy · GAME CORE)*
Logs a captured anomaly's "signature" and runs structured tests that whittle down its unknown properties — the field tool that builds a specimen's research profile for the Site.
*Image prompt:* A pistol-grip handheld scanner with a ring-shaped emitter aperture at the muzzle and a small top-mounted data readout, brushed-steel and off-white polymer, a spool of printed label tape at the base; thin cold-cyan scan-beam.

## Shared — containment & capture

**7. Portable Spatial Containment Sphere** *(canon · single-target capture · GAME CORE)*
A smooth black sphere the size of a fist; thrown at an anomalous organism, it folds space around the target and takes it **alive**. Defective units function as grenades — capture and kill are the same object, and the difference is quality control.
*Image prompt:* A seamless matte-black metal sphere resting in a foam-lined case, one hemisphere showing a faint recessed seam and a single dormant status light, hairline geometry hinting at an internal mechanism; dormant cold-white pinlight.

**8. Scranton Reality Anchor** *(canon · area suppression · GAME CORE)*
A deployable tripod pylon that raises the local Hume level, forcing anomalies to obey physics. When it powers up it visibly **drains color from the world** — the safety device that makes everything grey.
*Image prompt:* A waist-high tripod pylon with a humming cylindrical core wrapped in copper coils and cooling fins, ribbed cables anchoring to floor plates, stenciled hazard chevrons, the surrounding air faintly desaturated; cold-white core glow bleeding nearby color to grey.

**9. Scranton-Eamon Reality Sink** *(canon · area destabilization)*
The inverse of an anchor: it **lowers** the Hume level in a radius, thinning reality. A dangerous tactical dial — some anomalies can only be handled where physics is weaker.
*Image prompt:* A squat inverted-cone device planted on the floor drawing faint particulate inward toward a dark central aperture, gunmetal housing with warning placards, reality rippling at its rim; a void-black core ringed by a thin violet event horizon.

**10. Quarantine Lattice Emitters** *(original · area-denial quarantine · GAME CORE)*
A set of driven stakes that project a field-gradient wall, bounding an outbreak inside a perimeter you can hold — the primary tool for containing spreading, un-capturable anomalies like the Flesh that Hates.
*Image prompt:* Four rugged ground stakes with glowing emitter heads driven into a floor, thin translucent amber energy planes stretched between them forming a containment fence, olive-drab casings with fold-out barbed anchors; translucent amber barrier glow.

**11. Non-Physical Displacement Neutralizer (nPDN)** *(canon · enables ghost capture)*
A backpack projector that forces intangible, phasing, or incorporeal anomalies to become temporarily **solid** — so they can be shot, foamed, or crated.
*Image prompt:* A back-mounted emitter unit with a shoulder-slung directional wand ending in a wide grille, ribbed hoses connecting pack to wand, worn olive-drab with heat-scorched vents; pulsing pale-blue grille glow.

**12. Onto-Sealed Containment Crate** *(original · extraction · GAME CORE)*
The ruggedized crate a captured anomaly is loaded into for exfiltration — its exterior alarm switch left OFF for docile specimens, ON for the ones that shouldn't be forgotten. Getting this to extraction is how you **win**.
*Image prompt:* A heavy reinforced transport crate the size of a footlocker banded with steel ribs and hazard tape, a chunky exterior toggle labeled ARMED/SAFE, a small barred observation slit, carry handles and forklift channels; a single amber status lamp.

**13. Tesla Gate** *(canon — from the fan games, not the wiki · corridor control)*
A doorframe-mounted electrostatic barrier that arcs violently across a corridor on motion — area denial for controlling how anomalies and swarms move through a site.
*Image prompt:* A tall industrial doorway frame lined with copper electrodes and ceramic insulators, a lattice of blue-white arcs crackling across the opening, scorch marks on the surrounding wall, warning strobes; intense electric-blue arc glow.

**14. Martel Pitons** *(canon · traversal / extraction)*
Teleportation pylons deployed in pairs: anchor two and step instantly between them — your checkpoint-and-extraction lifeline through spatially hostile, looping anomalous terrain.
*Image prompt:* A pair of matte-black obelisk pylons about knee height, each with a glowing vertical seam and a splayed tripod base, a faint distortion shimmer between the paired tips, stenciled index numbers; thin cold-cyan seam glow with a spatial-warp shimmer.

## Combat specialist — suppression & ordnance

**15. Spelleater Rounds** *(canon · anti-thaumaturge ammunition)*
Beryllium-bronze rounds inscribed with a fractal warding glyph and blessed by ordained clerics; they punch through personal defensive charms and hit thaumaturgic entities where ordinary lead can't.
*Image prompt:* A row of large-caliber cartridges standing on end, bronze jackets etched with fine glowing fractal sigils, resting on a cloth beside an open ammo tin, one round toppled to show the engraved tip; faint amber-gold rune glow on the etching.

**16. TICK Rounds** *(canon · non-lethal capture-assist)*
Tactical Interrupter of Cognition and Kinetics — not a bullet but a device that unfolds mid-flight, hooks into tissue, and delivers continuous shock. Carries a **whitelist transmitter**, because the target might be your own psionic, turned against you.
*Image prompt:* A single specialized round shown cutaway to reveal folded barbed prongs and a tiny circuit core, displayed beside its intact form on a technical spec card marked with a small antenna icon, matte-grey polymer casing; tiny magenta status LED.

**17. Banishment Grenades** *(canon · anti-manifestation ordnance)*
A charge that generates a harmonic resonance keyed to the "music of the spheres," disrupting the physical vessels of entities not native to our reality.
*Image prompt:* A cylindrical grenade with concentric ring vents and a resonance-fork emitter at the top, brushed-steel body engraved with a waveform band and a pull ring, held in a gloved hand; faint amber sound-ripple emanating from the vents.

**18. Cold-Iron Scattergun Loadout** *(canon-inspired · anti-fae / thaumaturge)*
A break-action scattergun fed cold-iron and salt shot — folklore-grade countermeasures that fare better than lead against thaumaturges, fae-types, and things that resent worked iron.
*Image prompt:* A rugged break-action shotgun laid on a workbench beside loose cold-iron shot and a bandolier of red shells, worn walnut stock and blued barrels, salt spilling from an open shell; no anomalous glow, gritty and utilitarian.

**19. Suppression Foam Projector** *(original · non-lethal restraint)*
A tanked projector firing expanding, rapidly-hardening containment foam that immobilizes a single target in seconds without killing it — the humane way to pin an anomaly for crating.
*Image prompt:* A shoulder-carried tank-and-nozzle rig with a pressure gauge and a wide flared nozzle dripping pale foam, an olive-drab tank with hazard stencils and a coiled hose, a hardened blob of foam on the floor nearby; no glow, matte industrial.

## Psionic operative (Type Magenta)

**20. Argus-III Awareness Rig** *(canon · parasensory HUD)*
A head-worn sensor rig grafting a brief window of 360-degree parasensory awareness — you feel the room around you a fraction of a second before it happens. Issued to the sharpest operatives.
*Image prompt:* A sleek head-mounted array with a low-profile visor and small sensor pods ringing the temples and rear of the skull, matte-charcoal with thin exposed circuitry, a faint HUD reticle reflected in the visor; subtle magenta sensor-pod glow.

**21. Electrum Warding Hood** *(canon-inspired · psionic-defense PPE)*
An electrum-mesh-lined hood that attenuates psionic influence — standard anti-Type-Magenta protection, and, pointedly, rated against your own psionic if they ever slip the leash.
*Image prompt:* A heavy tactical hood and half-mask with a visible woven silver-gold metallic mesh lining, matte-black outer fabric, a chin-strap and a small grounding tab, displayed on a featureless head form; faint metallic electrum sheen, no active glow.

**22. Lambda-Wave Dampener** *(canon · electronics protection)*
A pole-mounted sink that absorbs the "lambda waves" psionic anomalies emit — the interference that fries electronics and scrambles instruments near a strong esper. Keeps the rest of your gear alive.
*Image prompt:* A slim vertical mast on a weighted base topped with a finned absorptive lattice, gunmetal with a spiral of copper wire and a small drain readout, standing at chest height; faint magenta static crawling over the lattice.

## Xenobiologist — biological handling

**23. Lambda-Model Bio-Isolation Suit** *(canon-inspired · outbreak PPE)*
A sealed full-body suit rated against biological, thaumaturgic, and radiological hazards — the standard kit for handling outbreaks and flesh-anomalies inside a quarantine lattice.
*Image prompt:* A full hazmat suit on a stand with a domed faceplate, ribbed rebreather hoses, taped seams and a chest-mounted filter box, off-white rubberized fabric grimed at knees and gloves, a Foundation logo on the shoulder; faint green filter-indicator light.

**24. Specimen Stasis Case** *(original · live capture · GAME CORE)*
A portable field case that keeps a captured biological anomaly alive, sedated, and monitored during exfiltration — the bridge between the containment sphere (#7) and the Site's research bay.
*Image prompt:* A hard-sided medical transport case opened to reveal a transparent stasis chamber with monitoring leads and a small vitals screen, foam padding, a fogged cold interior, brushed-aluminum shell with biohazard placards; soft cyan chamber underlight.

**25. Vector Cure Injector** *(original · parasite extraction · GAME CORE)*
A heavy pneumatic injector loaded with a bespoke gene-thaum "cure" that severs a parasite from its host and extracts it intact — the containment verb for host-riding anomalies like SCP-150.
*Image prompt:* A bulky pistol-grip injector with a thick glass ampoule of luminous serum and a broad pneumatic plunger, twin needle nozzles, gunmetal and medical-white housing with a dosage dial; glowing pale-green serum in the ampoule.

## Consumables

**26. Amnestic Dispenser** *(canon · cover-up)*
A cartridge dispenser of Class A–I amnestics — the Foundation's memory-wipe workhorse, handed out casually to any operative despite each dose being, technically, a contained anomaly nobody thinks about.
*Image prompt:* A compact belt-worn dispenser with a rotating drum of color-coded amnestic vials (A through I) and a single-dose ejection port, matte off-white clinical plastic with a class-key label, one amber vial ejected; clinical, faint amber vial tint.

**27. Class-W Mnestic Tabs** *(canon · anti-antimeme)*
Reverse-amnestic tablets that let the user perceive and retain antimemetic information for a limited time — the Antimemetics Division standard, and the only way to keep hold of an enemy you'd otherwise forget you were fighting.
*Image prompt:* A blister strip of stark-white tablets stamped with a small "W", half-pushed from foil backing, beside a plain amber pill bottle with a heavily-redacted label; clinical, high-contrast, no glow.

**28. RUSH Inhaler + DUBSTEP Earpiece** *(canon · combat stim)*
A paired combat stimulant: a nitro-amphetamine vapor that smells of lavender and grants low-light vision, and a binaural-beat earpiece that spikes adrenaline. The Foundation's combat stims come with a soundtrack.
*Image prompt:* A small lavender-tinted inhaler and a matte-black wireless earpiece resting together on a padded tray, the inhaler labeled RUSH with a dosage counter, the earpiece labeled DUBSTEP with a tiny waveform icon; a faint lavender vapor wisp.

## Signature & endgame

**29. CORVID-Class APV** *(canon · vehicle)*
The party's ride: a combat-ready anomalous armored minivan built by the Foundation's finest solid-state physicists and combat ontologists on a battered civilian chassis. Outdated now. Still reliable.
*Image prompt:* A boxy 1990s civilian minivan up-armored with bolt-on steel plating, a roof sensor mast and a Scranton-anchor pod on the rear bumper, matte-grey primer over faded factory paint, mismatched reinforced windows, parked under sodium lot lights; mostly mundane with one faint cyan anchor-pod glow.

**30. Coherence Anchor (Model 9191-C)** *(original · anti-slop · GAME CORE / ENDGAME)*
A specialized reality anchor tuned not against benders but against **generated** anomalies: it enforces high-fidelity consensus form, causing SCP-9191's ugly, half-rendered slop to destabilize and collapse where it can't hold a coherent shape. The Foundation's answer to an out-of-control generator.
*Image prompt:* A refined white-and-chrome pylon, cleaner and more precise than a battered Scranton anchor, its core projecting a crisp geometric lattice of "correct" edges into the air while a nearby malformed, glitching mass of mismatched textures visibly sharpens and crumbles at the lattice's edge; the anchor's field rendered as clean high-fidelity light dissolving a smeary, uncanny, low-resolution corruption.
