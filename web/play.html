    1 torsion · 2 bending · 3 axial · 4 direct high energy · T cycles tissue · [ ] impulse · R re-break

    The same subject, the same seed, four silhouettes. Sellán et al. (10.1145/3549540 §6) say their fault is the same regardless of the directionality of the impact, and name torsion as one of the missing cases. This is that case: a spiral with long sharp ends under torsion, a butterfly wedge under bending, a transverse break under an axial load, comminution under a direct blow.

    T is the other half. Cortical bone splits along its own grain — osteons run along the shaft and its toughness is far lower for a crack running with them — so it yields long sharp slivers; trabecular bone crushes, tolerating ~30 % strain against cortical's ~2 %, so it is clamped to three pieces however hard it is hit.

    Drop the impulse below the greenstick threshold and the bone bends instead of breaking: one fragment, no fault, and a permanent bow. Greenstick is an outcome, not a mode.

    [ ] impact energy · , . strain rate · T tissue · H the histogram

    The fragment count is not an artist constant. Grady's energy balance (10.1063/1.329934) gives a characteristic fragment size s = (24·G_c / (ρ·ε̇²))^(1/3), and the count is the subject's volume over s³ — so a faster load makes smaller pieces, cubically. The energy delivered is a second, hard ceiling: creating n fragments costs G_c per unit of new surface, and a blow cannot buy more than it brought.

    A pistol leaves two pieces and a wedge. A rifle comminutes. The histogram is drawn against Mott's distribution (10.1098/rspa.1947.0042) — many small, few large.

    A wall of stains swept over angle and speed. The aspect ratio is sin θ — the impact-angle relation the whole of bloodstain-pattern analysis rests on (10.1520/jfs2003224). Spines come from Knock & Davison's angle-inclusive law, 0.76 · We^0.5 · sin³θ at R² ≈ 0.9 (10.1111/j.1556-4029.2007.00505.x), and satellites appear past Mundo's splash threshold K = We^0.5 · Re^0.25 ≥ 57.7.

    B is the argument, in one keypress. It shows the same wall drawn the old way — four baked textures picked by seed % 4 — and the arithmetic beside it: the probability that four consecutive stains contain a repeat is 90.6 %, so the expected first visible repeat is the fourth stain. Authoring more textures moves the number and not the shape of the problem.

    1 impact spatter · 2 arterial · 3 cast-off · 4 expirated · 5 drip trail · 6 transfer · C clear

    The six SWGSTAIN / ASB TR-033 classes, as six mechanisms rather than one cone at six intensities. The arterial one fires once per systole, a handful of large discrete stains along an arc whose reach falls as the body loses pressure — not another isotropic spray.

    Cast-off is tangential, and that is the measured fact rather than the folklore (Williams et al., 10.1111/1556-4029.13855): the drop leaves along the tip's instantaneous velocity, never radially outward, and its diameter is inversely proportional to tip speed. Adam (10.1016/j.forsciint.2019.109934) measured the pendant volume at 150 µL, so a weapon swung repeatedly sheds less each swing — watch the counter.

    The drip trail's spacing encodes walking speed. The transfer wipe spends a conserved budget, so a dragged body runs out of blood — and the shape of that is worth watching rather than guessing at: each contact moves min(load, transfer_rate) out of the budget, so the marks hold their full width while there is blood to give, the last one is narrower because only a fraction was left, and then there are no more. Not a fade: a supply running out. That budget is what makes a pattern tell a story.

    ← → shear rate · ↑ ↓ hematocrit · [ ] age · Space release a rivulet

    Blood is a shear-thinning yield-stress fluid, and the two things you can see follow from that and nothing else. Carreau–Yasuda with Cho & Kensey's constants gives the first: a fast rivulet is thin and races, a slow one thickens and beads.

    The second is the interesting one. A yield-stress fluid moves only while the driving stress exceeds its own yield stress, so a rivulet arrests mid-wall the moment the rising yield stress overtakes the falling drive. That is what clotting is — the same comparison, at a different age — so the clot, the arrested rivulet and the pool that stopped creeping are one mechanism rather than three special cases. The plot shows both curves crossing.

    ← → scrub the age · H humidity · Space play

    Scrub the age axis and watch four channels move at once. The colour walks oxyhaemoglobin → methaemoglobin → hemichrome (Bremmer et al., 10.1016/j.forsciint.2011.07.027) — three stops, not two, which is why old blood is a brown rather than a dark red. The gloss collapses; the rim dries first, matte at the edge while the centre is still wet (Smith, Nicloux & Brutin, 10.1038/s41598-020-65465-4); and the crust cracks late into a craquelure.

    Push the humidity past 50 % and a serum halo appears outside the pool, where the serum phase-separates and spreads (Laan et al., 10.1016/j.forsciint.2016.08.005). Below the threshold there is none at all, at any age — a refusal rather than a very small number.

    The channel that matters most is not a colour: moistness, not hue, is what drives disgust (Oum, Lieberman & Aylward, 10.1080/02699931.2010.496997), so wetness is a specular channel here rather than a tint.

At Stylised the emitter still fires, on the same tick, in the same direction, at the same magnitude — it swaps the blood palette for a spark. Vermintide 2's gore-off deleted the channel and made the game harder to read; Gears of War 4 replaced blood with sparks and kept the hit confirmation.

    F holds down the flash button, and the meter shows the gate refusing the fourth flash inside any one second — WCAG 2.1 SC 2.3.1, technique G19, whose safe harbour is 3 Hz. A gore framework is mechanically a saturated-red flash generator, so the limiter lives in it and cannot be configured upward.

    A draws the aim-exclusion cone: decals that would land on the reticle are refused, because gaze concentrates at screen centre while aiming. Ten degrees, which is the visual-field unit WCAG itself uses — not thirty, which is folklore.

    click shoot the body · Space a burst · G gravity direction · D the digest

    Blood lands where the subject was hit in UV space, runs down the actual geometry, pools where the surface turns, and dries in place. Texture-space blood accumulation is AAA-only — The Last of Us Part II's blood and wound render targets — and everywhere it exists it is a GPU render target.

    Which is why nobody can hash one. This one is CPU-authoritative, so the digest on screen is a fact about the canvas: paint the same sequence twice and it matches, move one hit by a single texel and it does not. That makes it the first wetmap that can be a golden.

    Dry paint does not move. Only wet paint drips or spreads, which is what makes a run stop where it stopped.

    Space spill · G grab and drag · T tear the mesentery · R reset

    XPBD Cosserat-style strands: guts spill with weight, coil on the floor, stay tethered by the mesenteric membrane, and then tear. The formulation is Deul et al.'s direct position-based solver for stiff rods (10.1111/cgf.13326) over Bergou et al.'s discrete elastic rods (10.1145/1399504.1360662).

    Deterministic, and that is a design constraint rather than a happy accident: a fixed substep count, a fixed iteration count, a fixed constraint order and no early-out. A tear is monotone — once a mesentery link parts it is skipped forever — so it cannot heal and the solver cannot oscillate.

    Orbits on its own.

    Three cut limbs — a thigh, a trunk, a skull — and every cap is anatomy: skin, subcutaneous fat, muscle, cortical bone and marrow at the depths measured on living adults by ultrasound (Akkus 2012 10.4172/2155-6156.1000216, Derraik 2014 10.1371/journal.pone.0086637, Abe 2015 10.1177/1742271X14554678, Dimitrova 2018 10.5603/fm.a2017.0114). Depth below the skin is exact on a convex cell — the minimum over its skin planes — and lands in a second UV channel; the strip it samples is baked once per region, at physical scale, so a 2 mm fat lobule is 2 mm on a thigh and 2 mm on a finger.

    CPU-side and frozen: the strip is a pure function of the thickness table, a size and a seed, and its digest is a golden.

    Space strike the bottom-left cell, harder each press · R rebuild

    A wall of cells with one weak seam. Sellán et al.'s fracture modes (10.1145/3549540): the sparse, mass-orthonormal modes of the cell graph are computed once, an impact is projected onto them, and every face whose two cells still agree is glued back. The seam gives first whichever corner is struck — the property Voronoi prefracture cannot have.

    Deterministic to the bit: fixed-sweep Jacobi initialisation, a fixed number of ADMM steps, no convergence test anywhere.

    The flagship: one body, every injury, one plugin. Three shots at one spot peel the torso to cortex and the fracture modes part the body; a blow leaves a bruise that ages through Stam's kinetics (10.1007/s11517-010-0647-5) in time-lapse; a hot iron leaves a burn whose degree Henriques' damage integral decides (Gowrishankar et al., 10.1186/1475-925x-3-42); a slash gapes and drips.
